# Menu image credits

Menu photographs are the restaurant's own photos from the OrderX ordering menu (`res.us.disoo.com`). They are shown only on items that have a photo there.

Hero and decorative food photos shipped with the site (`hero-butter-chicken.png`, `hero-chicken-tikka.png`, `hero-butter-paneer.png`, `karahi.png`, and `pizza-coming-soon.png`) are unchanged local assets.
